Collabify

An iOS app to match small businesses with influencers for paid promotions. It's like a dating app for brand deals.

The idea

Small businesses want influencer marketing but don't know where to start. Small influencers want paid deals but can't find them. Collabify would match the two sides, handle payment in the app, and take a small fee.

What I built

I built an iOS prototype in Xcode with SwiftUI: profiles for both sides, the matching flow, and separate dashboards for businesses and creators. To be clear, I don't know Swift. I built it the way I build everything, by directing AI, testing what it made and pushing back until it worked.

My honest take

When I seriously pressure-tested this, it didn't hold up. Collabstr, Aspire and JoinBrands already do this, and I had zero real evidence that businesses would pay for my version. So I paused it.

It still wasn't wasted. It was my first time building an iOS app, and I learned to look for proof of demand before building anything. My instinct now is "something like this probably already exists, so go find it first."

What's next

Parked. A local, Auburn-only version is the one angle that still looked alive.
